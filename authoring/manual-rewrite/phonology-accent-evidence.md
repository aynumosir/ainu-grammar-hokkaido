| Accent may distinguish words | Nakagawa 2024 pp. 48–51; Satō 2008 pp. 14–15; Shiraishi 2022 §5.1; Nakagawa and Fukazawa 2022 §3.1. Prior analyses with published examples. | Agreement for the accentual varieties; no basis for extending to all dialects. | Give Saru pairs and identify dialect scope. |
| Default placement | Nakagawa 2024 p. 50; Satō 2008 p. 15; Shibatani 1990 §2.3 p. 12; Shiraishi 2022 §5.1. Prior analysis. | Closed initial syllable attracts accent; after an open initial syllable, second-syllable accent is usual. Lexical and morphological exceptions remain. | State as the default for the relevant varieties. |
| Rising kernel | Nakagawa 2024 pp. 48–49. Prior analysis and paired examples. | Location of the rise distinguishes words; later fall need not. | Use the author's kamúynomi example; no reconstructed community judgment. |
| Weight analysis | Shiraishi 2022 §5.1. Prior analysis. | Light (C)V versus heavy (C)VC supports an iambic account. | Attribute the weight-based analysis; do not invent a syllable-versus-mora adjudication. |
| Dialect grouping | Nakagawa and Fukazawa 2022 §3.1; Nakagawa 2024 pp. 49–51; Refsing 1986 §10.6 p. 73. Prior analysis. | Refsing describes the ordinary rules in Shizunai; Nakagawa gives variable tokens by the same speaker and favors lack of lexical contrast. | Yakumo accent | Nakagawa 2024 pp. 50–51; Nakagawa and Fukazawa 2022 §3.1. Prior analysis. | Third-syllable accent when the first two syllables are open; lexical exceptions. The sources differ on hopuni. | Use their shared tattoo example sinuyé, avoid the disputed hopuni token. |
| Minimal pairs | Nakagawa 2024 p. 48: nísap/nisáp, nína/niná; Shiraishi 2022 §5.1: nísap/nisáp, útur/utúr. | Functional load | Shiraishi 2022 §5.1 describes a handful of pairs; Nakagawa 2024 p. 51 lists exceptions without quantifying the lexicon. | No measured functional-load statistic in these passages. | Describe contrast without inventing a rate or a closed exception list. |
| Sakhalin correspondence | Nakagawa 2024 p. 51; Nakagawa and Fukazawa 2022 §3.1; Bugaeva 2012 p. 467. | Compound accent | Shiraishi 2022 §5.2. Prior analysis. | First-element priority competes with iambic placement. | Cross-reference chapter 26; do not claim one nucleus for every morphological word. |
